Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

The Monkey King Reborn ("the App") is an ad-free, offline single-player game. We respect your privacy and do not collect, use, or share any personal data.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, address, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Any data from children under 13

Local Storage Only

The App may use standard local storage (e.g. PlayerPrefs) on your device solely to save your in-game progress. This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics providers, ad networks, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated policy will be posted here with a new effective date.
